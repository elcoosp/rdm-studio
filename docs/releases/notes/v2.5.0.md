## Detailed Engineering Changelog

### 1. EN 1993-1-8 Steel Connection Engine & Studio (`crates/rdm-eurocode`, `ConnectionStudioModal`)
- **Analytical Eurocode 3 Part 1-8 Solver (`crates/rdm-eurocode`):** Implements bolted end-plate beam-to-column and splice connection checks against hand-calculated benchmarks (`34c6a4e`), evaluating bolt-row tension/shear interaction, weld throat resistance, and initial rotational stiffness `Sj,ini` for rigid/semi-rigid/pinned joint classification.
- **Interactive *Assemblages* Studio & ⌘K Command:** Adds the EC3-1-8 Connection Studio modal (`4402c5b`, `be7beff`) with live utilization ratios, failure-mode breakdown, and instant recalculation via the WASM worker (`f9be1c7`).

### 2. EN 1991-2 Moving Loads, Influence Lines & Inter-Storey Drift (`crates/rdm-solver`, `Step 3–4`)
- **Axle-Train Convoys & `uz` Influence Lines (`9da2e65`, `afa4f5b`):** Solves moving vehicle/tandem axle trains (`LM1`, custom convoys) along continuous beams and bridge girders, computing exact `uz` influence lines, pattern load triggers (`29a43f2`), and critical min/max internal force envelopes.
- **SLS Inter-Storey Drift, Member Groups & Inclined Supports (`1ffeac0`, `6e72da0`, `fd5fc48`):** Evaluates inter-storey horizontal drift ratios per level (`6007ee7`), adds member group management with grouped uniform-profile optimization (`138b896`), custom material definitions, and arbitrary inclined boundary support angles with 3D roller tilt rendering (`4aa5363`).

### 3. Bidirectional BIM Interoperability & M12 Calculation Reports (`Step 1`, `Step 5`, `docxExport`)
- **IFC4 / SAF BIM Import & Export (`6a20523`, `5939efe`):** Imports and exports structural analytical models with cross-section, material, support, and load-case mapping directly in Step 1.
- **M12 Report Chapters & Native `.docx` Tables (`ec8bc43`, `b5a66df`):** Registers EC3-1-8 joint verification sheets, convoy envelope tables, and drift summaries into the client-side A4/PDF and `.docx` calculation note pipeline across `FR`, `EN`, `DE`, `ES`, and `PT-BR`.

> [!TIP]
> **Zero-Cloud Local-First Execution:** Open the [v2.5.0 reference model directly in RDM Studio](https://rdm-studio-app.vercel.app/geometry?template=portal-frame&lang=en) — the Rust/WASM solver (`f64` precision), EN 1993-1-8 connection engine, axle-train solver, and `.docx`/PDF report generators run 100% locally in your browser.
