## Action families

The **Charges** step organizes actions in EN 1990 families. Each action carries a
γG/γQ factor set and the ψ values needed by the combination recipes.

| Family | Source | Generator inputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G — permanent | self-weight + superimposed | densities from the material catalog |
| Q — imposed | EN 1991-1-1 category | category A…K, qk, Qk |
| W — wind | EN 1991-1-4 | vb, c/cd, terrain class, cpe zones |
| S — snow | EN 1991-1-3 | sk, μ1/μ2 shapes, altitude |
| T — thermal | EN 1991-1-5 | uniform ΔT, gradient ΔT |
| Acc — acceleration | naval / lifting / seismic | (ax, ay, az) × g |
| LM1 — moving | EN 1991-2 | TS ×2 axles + UDL lanes |

## Wind: from vb to pressure

The wind generator derives the peak pressure from the basic wind velocity:

```text
vb   = vb,0 · cdir · cseason           (EN 1991-1-4 §4.2)
qp   = (1 + 7·Iv) · ½ · ρ · vm²        (peak velocity pressure, §4.5)
we   = qp · cpe                        (net pressure on a surface, §5.2)
```

Enter `vb,0` for the French zone, the terrain category (0…IV) and the cpe pressure
coefficients per face; the generator splits windward, leeward and uplift loads onto
the members you attach them to.

## Snow and thermal

The snow generator applies the shape coefficients μ1/μ2 (monopitch, duopitch,
valleys) to the characteristic value `sk` of the site. The thermal generator creates
uniform expansion (`ΔT` on all members) and gradient cases (deck heating) — the
`R26`/`R27` benchmarks certify the resulting self-stressed states against the
analytical solutions.

## Acceleration fields (ax, ay, az) × g

Naval, lifting and seismic-adjacent environments enter as **volumetric inertia**:
each component is a fraction of g applied to the whole structure and to attached
masses. Components become independent actions (`Acc-x`, `Acc-y`, `Acc-z`) with both
gisements generated, so `ELU = G + 1,3·az + 0,4·ay` and its mirrored twin appear
automatically. See the naval cradle tutorial for a complete worked example.

## LM1 moving loads

The EN 1991-2 LM1 generator places the tandem system (two 300 kN axles per lane) and
the 9 kN/m² uniform load per lane with the adjustable lane count and eccentricity.
Because the tandem positions are controlled, the solver sweeps the convoy along the
span and reports the envelope — the position that maximizes `My` and the one that
maximizes `Vz` are both kept for the verification step.

## The two EN 1990 recipes

The combination table computes both statutory recipes:

```text
6.10    : Σ γG,j·Gk,j + γQ,1·Qk,1 + Σ γQ,i·ψ0,i·Qk,i
6.10a   : Σ γG,j·Gk,j + γ0,1·γQ,1·ψ0,1·Qk,1 + Σ γ0,i·γQ,i·ψ0,i·Qk,i
6.10b   : ξ·Σ γG,j·Gk,j + γQ,1·Qk,1 + Σ γQ,i·ψ0,i·Qk,i
```

The French National Annex values (γG = 1,35, γQ = 1,50, ψ0 tables, ξ = 0,85) are
pre-wired and certified by benchmarks `E05`–`E07`. Switch the recipe selector to
compare 6.10 against 6.10a/b side by side — the governing envelope is what the
verification step uses.
