This tutorial guides you through the **entire 5-step workspace** on a real steel portal frame: the 18 m single-span frame with HEA 200 columns and IPE 300 rafter, fixed-pinned bases, gravity load, and EN 1990 combination `1.35·G + 1.50·Q`. ## Step 1 — Geometry: Load the template

Open the studio at `rdm-studio-app.vercel.app`. The gallery (header → gallery icon) lists all 14 catalog models by sector. Choose **Building & Framing → Steel Portal Frame 18 m**. The model opens with:

- 2 columns: HEA 200, 5.2 m each
- 1 rafter: IPE 300, 18.0 m span
- Magnetic snap grid: 50 mm (adjustable to 10/100 mm)
- Nodes at column bases and haunch connections

## Step 2 — Sections & Mass

At **Géométrie**, the left inspector shows every member with profile, length, mass, and mass share. The total mass is ~2.84 t. Click the rafter to try `IPE 330` — the mass, EC3 rate badge, and 3D silhouette update instantly thanks to the persistent stage and reactive store.

## Step 3 — Supports & Loads

At **Liaisons**: Column A (left) is **Pinned** (releases Rx, Ry, Rz), Column B (right) is **Fixed** (all 6 DOF fixed). The anti-roll restraint on the pinned base locks `Rx` — required for any collinear X-frame.

At **Charges**: One uniform gravity load case on the rafter, vector `{ x: 0, y: 0, z: -1.5 }` kN/m. The combination is `1.35·G + 1.50·Q` (EN 1990 6.10). Switch to `6.10a/b` to see ψ-weighted combinations.

## Step 4 — Solver & Results

Press **Calculer**. The Cholesky LDLᵀ factorization solves the 6-DOF system in < 20 ms. The **Calcul** step shows:

- Deformed shape with 5-tier thermal spectrum (configurable min/max)
- uPlot diagrams: `My` (bending), `Vz` (shear), `N` (axial)
- EC3 verification table: Nb,Rd, Mc,Rd, Vpl,Rd, LTB χLT per member
- Cutting plane slider (0 to 1.0 L) with synchronized 2D section cut

## Step 5 — Export Calculation Note

At **Rapport**, the A4 vector PDF (Takumi renderer, embedded Inter + JetBrains Mono) contains:

- Cover with firm branding (Pro edition)
- Reactions table ΣRz, ΣMx, ΣMy
- Member-by-member EC3 rates with clause references (EN 1993-1-1 6.61, 6.62, C1)
- SLS deflection checks against L/250 and L/300
- SHA-256 fingerprint of the solved model
- CalcTrace: every rate expands to a KaTeX step-by-step formula

Generate PDF, `.docx`, or copy tables as TSV.

## Key Takeaways

- **Templates are production-ready**: supports, loads, combinations pre-wired.
- **Solver is local & instant**: < 20 ms for this model size.
- **Every number is traceable**: CalcTrace links rates to normative clauses.
- **Report is vector & signed-ready**: SHA-256 fingerprint certifies the exact model.
